## How to Select the Right Pallet for Your Operation

Before ordering, consider the entire journey from loading to final delivery. A pallet that fits the product but cannot be handled efficiently by your equipment may create avoidable problems during daily operations.

- **Measure the cargo:** Record the product's length, width, and height, including any protruding sections.
- **Calculate the total load:** Include the weight of the product, cartons, protective materials, and other items placed on the pallet.
- **Review weight distribution:** Identify concentrated loads or uneven shapes that may require additional support.
- **Check entry access:** Confirm whether forklifts or pallet jacks need access from two sides or all four.
- **Plan the stacking arrangement:** Determine whether pallets will be stored individually, stacked, or placed in racking systems.
- **Consider handling frequency:** Repeated movement may call for a different construction than a pallet intended for a single shipment.
- **Identify shipping requirements:** Check carrier specifications, destination restrictions, and export requirements where applicable.

Sharing these details with your packaging supplier can help clarify which pallet specifications need to be considered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What are custom wooden pallets used for?
They provide a wooden base for handling, storing, and transporting products that may require dimensions or support arrangements different from standard pallets.

### 2. Can wooden pallets be made in different sizes?
Custom pallet dimensions may be available depending on the supplier's capabilities. Provide your cargo measurements and required footprint when requesting a quote.

### 3. What is the difference between two-way and four-way entry?
Two-way pallets allow forklift entry from two opposing sides, while four-way configurations are designed to allow entry from all four sides. The actual access depends on the pallet's construction.

### 4. How do I determine the pallet's required load capacity?
Consider the total cargo weight, how the weight is distributed, how the pallet will be supported, and whether it will be moved or stored in a rack. Confirm the appropriate capacity with the supplier.

### 5. Are custom wooden pallets suitable for industrial equipment?
They can be considered for suitable industrial loads when the pallet is designed around the equipment's dimensions, weight, support points, and handling requirements.

### 6. Can wooden pallets be reused?
Yes, if they remain structurally sound and are suitable for the next load. Inspect them for damaged boards, loose fasteners, contamination, and other defects before reuse.

### 7. Do international shipments require treated wooden pallets?
Certain international shipments using solid wood packaging must comply with ISPM 15 and destination-country requirements. Confirm whether treatment and appropriate marking are required for your shipment.

### 8. Should I choose a custom pallet or a standard pallet?
A standard pallet may be suitable when its dimensions and capacity meet your needs. A custom design is worth considering when the cargo is oversized, unusually shaped, or incompatible with available standard footprints.

### 9. What information is needed to request a pallet quote?
Provide the required dimensions, cargo weight, entry configuration, application, estimated quantity, and any shipping or export requirements.

### 10. Can wooden pallets be used with stretch film and other packaging materials?
Yes. Stretch film, straps, corner protection, blocking, and other suitable materials may be used to secure a load, depending on its shape, weight, and transportation requirements.
